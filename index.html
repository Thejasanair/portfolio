01 — About

Working at the numerical edge of general relativity

I am a physics graduate from Kerala with a working interest in how strong gravity behaves when it cannot be solved by hand. My MSc project used the Einstein Toolkit to evolve the density field of a neutron star forward in time, under the supervision of Dr. Krishna Mohan Parattu at IIT Mandi, and I spent a good part of that project simply comparing how numerical error grows on coarser and finer grids.

Outside of that project, I have looked at how galactic bars form and how central bulges influence that process, and — further back — measured the wavelength of LED light using Newton's rings, my first real experiment. I am currently preparing for research and doctoral positions in gravitation and related areas of theoretical physics.

02 — Research

Research experience

Apr 2023 – Aug 2023 MSc project

Numerical Relativity using Einstein Toolkit

Supervised by Dr. Krishna Mohan Parattu, IIT Mandi

Studied the ADM formulation and the broader machinery of numerical relativity, then used the Einstein Toolkit to evolve the density profile of a neutron star in time. Compared how numerical error propagates across a range of grid resolutions.

2022 – 2023 MSc mini project

Galaxy Bars

Supervised by Dr. Vinu Vikram, Central University of Kerala

Studied the formation of bars in disk galaxies and examined how the presence and size of a central bulge affects bar formation.

2020 – 2021 BSc project

Wavelength of LEDs via Newton's Rings

Supervised by C.V. Sandhya, Mahatma Gandhi College

Calculated the wavelengths of blue, green, and red LEDs using the Newton's-ring interference experiment.
